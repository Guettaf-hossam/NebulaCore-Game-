STEGANOGRAPHY · HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT ✖
STEGANOGRAPHY_less_data : HIDDEN ART

Steganography is the practice of concealing a message within another non-secret medium. Unlike cryptography, it hides the very existence of the data.

Image Steganography: Least Significant Bit (LSB) embedding — altering pixel bytes slightly to encode secret text or images. Invisible to the naked eye.

Audio Steganography: Hiding data within audio frequencies (echo hiding, phase encoding).

Text Steganography: Using whitespace, invisible characters (zero-width spaces) or format tricks.

Network Steganography: Covert channels in TCP/IP headers or timing patterns.

✨ In the Nebula Core archives, ancient engineers hid fragments of the virus counter-code inside landscape images. The key was always visible but never noticed — like whispers in a storm.
